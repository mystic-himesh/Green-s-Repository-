04 · from my heart

I wrote you
a letter.

for you, always ♡

Dear Green!,

I don't know if a website can say what I haven't been able to say properly, but I wanted to try.

I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the moments where I hurt you, for the things I could have handled differently, and for the times when I didn't understand what you were trying to tell me.

Being apart made me realize a lot. Some things become much clearer when you don't have them right in front of you.

I don't want this letter to erase the past. I just want you to know that I remember it, I've thought about it, and I've learned from it.

And somehow, after everything, we found our way back to each other.

I'm genuinely grateful for that.

I can't promise that we'll never misunderstand each other again. But I can promise that I'll try harder to listen, communicate, understand, and appreciate what we have.

Thank you for being here again.

I'm sorry.
And I'm really glad it's us again. ♡

— Himesh
